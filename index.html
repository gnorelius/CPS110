About SUNY New Paltz

The State University of New York at New Paltz is a public university located in the scenic Hudson Valley of New York. The campus combines a strong academic environment with opportunities for creativity, research, leadership, athletics, and community involvement.

Academic Opportunities

Arts & Design

Explore creative disciplines including visual arts, design, music, theatre, and related fields.

Science & Technology

Develop skills through programs in science, engineering, technology, mathematics, and computer-related fields.

Business & Social Sciences

Prepare for careers and graduate study through programs spanning business, psychology, education, and the social sciences.
